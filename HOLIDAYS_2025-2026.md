## 🎄 Special Christmas Season Features

The Philippines has the **longest Christmas season in the world**, starting in September! For this display, we've included:

### December 1 - Official Start
- "🎄 Start of Christmas Season" message appears

### December 16-23 - Simbang Gabi
- Daily countdown messages for the traditional 9-day dawn mass
- Each day tracked individually

### December 24 - Noche Buena
- Special Christmas Eve message

### December 25-26 - Christmas Days
- Christmas Day and Boxing Day

### December 27-29 - Christmas Week
- Extended celebration period

### December 30 - Rizal Day
- Philippine national hero remembrance

### December 31 - New Year's Eve
- Transition to the new year

## 📅 How It Works

The display automatically detects today's date and shows:
- **If it's a holiday**: Shows the holiday name with emoji
- **If it's a regular day**: Shows "📅 Regular day"
- **If multiple holidays**: Shows both (e.g., Korean + Philippine holiday on same day)

The calendar is automatically checked every second, so it updates at midnight without needing to refresh!

---

## 🔧 Updating the Calendar

To add more holidays, edit: `/Users/icanacademy/teacher-attendance-display/client/app.js`

Look for:
- Line 73: `koreanHolidays` object
- Line 110: `philippineHolidays` object

Format:
```javascript
'YYYY-MM-DD': '🎉 Holiday Name',
```
